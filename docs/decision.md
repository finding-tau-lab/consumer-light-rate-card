# Loss-leader carton vs 40 lb UPS Ground

Decision: $9.99 did not cover this book. $14.99 cut the per-box hole by about $5 and did not close zones 6–8, which held the volume.

Unit: paid US basic consumer package, UPS Ground, 27 x 17 x 12 in, 9 lb actual, 40 lb DIM, origin 84098. Sheet-only was USPS and is out. HI/AK out.

Population: 1,548 paid Light orders. 1,330 zoned. 218 had no shipping or billing zip (mostly CA, NY, FL, TX) and are excluded. Zoned split: 488 at $9.99, 842 at $14.99. Zones 5–8 are 1,196 of 1,330.

Cost model: published 40 lb Ground daily list times a discount, plus $3.60 residential. Not an invoice. Fuel excluded.

Per zoned box, charged minus estimated cost:
- pay 70% of list: -$22.37 at $9.99, -$17.21 at $14.99
- pay 50% of list: -$14.15 at $9.99, -$9.04 at $14.99
- pay 30% of list: -$5.94 at $9.99, -$0.87 at $14.99
- pay 20% of list (recalled account discount, unverified): -$1.83 at $9.99, +$3.22 at $14.99

What changes in the rate card: this is the consumer parcel card only. A zone floor on Light past zone 4, or kill Light past zone 5. Enterprise healthcare shipped freight under a different process and is out of this table.
